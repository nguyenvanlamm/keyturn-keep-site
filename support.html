How to play

Tin toys march down the quilt from the toy chest toward your pillow fort. Drag along a seam to slide its brass key under the march: every toy that crosses turns around and marches back up as yours, knocking over toys on their way down. Get enough toys into the chest to shut the lid before the fort loses its 20 pillows. Tap the quilt to drop a pillow on the closest toys. The full rules are in Settings → How to play.

Purchases

All Quilts ($3.99, one time) opens Flying Geese and Log Cabin; the Paint Set ($1.99, one time) adds paints and quilt patterns; a Toy Bag ($1.99) holds 5 Spare Keys and 5 Pillow Piles. Bought something before, or on another device with the same store account? Open Settings → Restore Purchases.

Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
