Turn the march around.

Every soldier you have was theirs a second ago

Keys, not cannons

Drag a turnkey gate along its seam to meet the march. Each toy that crosses is wound backwards: its key turns brass and it heads back up the quilt. A key holds ten turns and winds back up while you wait.

One move, two jobs

A turned toy marches back up its own column and knocks over the next toy coming down, one for one. Where you place a key decides who gets stopped and which lane your counter-march clears.

Toys with tempers

Mice walk straight in. Beetles crack their shell on the first gate and only turn on the second. Bears are slow, need four turns of a key, and knock four pillows off the fort if they reach it.

A minute or two a level

Fill the chest until the lid shuts before the fort loses its 20 pillows. Half gates turn every second toy, a tap drops a pillow on the closest toys, and three sewn buttons wait on every quilt.

On the quilt

How it plays

  1. The chest springs openGroups of grey tin toys march down the quilt toward your pillow fort.
  2. Slide a keyDrag along a seam to move its brass key under the march. Toys that cross turn brass and head back up.
  3. Shut the lidGet enough toys into the chest before the fort runs out of pillows.

Questions

Is it free?

The first quilt, Patchwork, is free with all 15 of its levels. Flying Geese and Log Cabin open with one purchase, All Quilts ($3.99). The Paint Set and the Toy Bag are optional.

Are there ads?

No. The game has no ads and no ad networks.

Do you collect any data?

No. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress and purchases are saved on your device only. See the privacy policy.

Does it work offline?

Yes. You only need a connection to buy or restore something in the Shop.

Can every level be won without boosters?

Yes. Every one of the 45 levels is beaten by a test bot in the game's automated checks without using a booster.
