# Keyturn Keep site

Landing, privacy and support pages for **Keyturn Keep**, a reverse-siege game for iPhone: slide brass keys along a
quilt's seams and wind-up tin toys turn around to fight for your pillow fort.

Live at https://nguyenvanlamm.github.io/keyturn-keep-site/ (GitHub Pages from `main`, no build step).

- `index.html`: landing page
- `privacy.html`: App Store privacy policy URL
- `support.html`: App Store support URL
